Battleship Rolling Card

Close any wormhole that passes battleships with one roller, and always end on the home side. A hole's real mass is up to 10% above or below its listed total, so every plan below is worked out for the whole range, not just the listed number.

Rules

  • Start on a fresh hole: stable, nothing logged through it yet.
  • → OUT goes to the far side, ← BACK comes home. Passes always alternate.
  • Any ← BACK jump may close the hole early. That's fine: you're home.
  • Two battleships can share the work. Never have both on the far side.

Checking the hole

At a ◆ check, Show Info on the wormhole and follow the lane that matches:

  • Stable half or more of its mass left
  • Reduced under half left, not critical
  • Critical under 10% left

⚠ No spare margin. These out jumps are safe for every possible hole mass, but only just: in the worst case the hole has exactly as much mass left as the jump uses. They stay safe as long as your battleship really is 200,000 t cold and 300,000 t hot. Weigh your fit before rolling; if it is heavier, use the mass dialog in Tripwire instead of this card.

Still open after the last step? It has almost no mass left. Don't send the battleship out again: let it expire, or close it with a cheap frigate.

Hole already used? If anything has gone through it, this card's numbers no longer apply. Log the jumps and open the hole's mass dialog in Tripwire: How to roll this hole plans from the logged mass and the current state.

K162s take the type of the hole's other side. Hole masses come from Tripwire's wormhole table. Plans were checked against every real hole mass in the ±10% range.
